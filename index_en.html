About

01 // TECHNICAL VISION

I specialize in the design of Service-Oriented Architectures (SOA) and Microservices, accommodating the coexistence of legacy systems and Cloud environments (AWS). My development approach prioritizes technical efficiency; I am a firm advocate of native web standards (Vanilla JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3) to build high-performance interfaces, avoiding unnecessary framework overhead when solutions require agility and low resource consumption.

Beyond code, I have spent several years dedicated to systems governance. I have designed and implemented institutional development methodologies and led compliance control areas, ensuring that the software lifecycle meets methodological, auditing, and operational stability standards.

AI-Assisted Development

Integration of agent-assisted development workflows (Antigravity, OpenCode, reasoning models such as Gemini and Claude), combining vibe coding for scoped iterations with the rigorous implementation of harnesses, guardrails, and test strategies in critical architectures.
